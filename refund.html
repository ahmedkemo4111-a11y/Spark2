Refund Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled for subscriptions and purchases made through the Spark platform ("Service") operated by Ahmed Kamel.

Spark is currently in a free trial period. No payments are being processed at this time. This policy will apply when paid plans are introduced.

1. Subscription Refunds

If you are charged for a subscription and are not satisfied with the Service, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your payment date. Refund requests made after 14 days will not be eligible for a refund, except where required by applicable law.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us at contact@sparkcrm.cloud with the following information:

  • Your account email address
  • The date of the charge
  • The reason for your refund request

We will process your request within 5 business days and respond by email.

3. Processing Refunds

Approved refunds will be returned to the original payment method used at the time of purchase. Refunds may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement depending on your bank or card issuer.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following situations:

  • Requests made more than 14 days after the payment date
  • Accounts that have been terminated due to violation of our Terms of Service
  • Partial month subscriptions after the 14-day window

5. Free Trial

No payment is required during the free trial period. You will not be charged unless you choose to upgrade to a paid plan.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions about refunds? Contact us at contact@sparkcrm.cloud.
