FAQ

Common questions

Who is Spark for? +
Spark is built for SAP consultants and SAP partner firms who need to run structured outreach to potential clients. If you're managing a list of prospects and sending personalized messages to book meetings, Spark is designed for you.
Is my data private? Can other users see my prospects? +
Yes, completely. Each Spark account is fully isolated. Your prospects, messages, and activity are only visible to you when you're logged in. No other user can access your data.
Does Spark send messages automatically? +
No. Spark helps you draft and organize your messages, but you always send them yourself through your own channels (phone, LinkedIn, email). Spark is a drafting and tracking tool, not an automation tool.
What does the onboarding profile do? +
When you sign up, Spark asks you a few questions about your company — what services you offer, your value proposition, your preferred tone, and your approach to outreach. This profile is then used to personalize every message draft Spark generates for you.
Is Spark free to use? +
Yes, Spark is currently free during our trial period. Paid plans are coming soon. Sign up now to lock in early access before pricing is introduced.
What information do I need to get started? +
Just your email and a password to create an account. Then a quick 5-step onboarding to set up your company profile. The whole setup takes under 5 minutes.
