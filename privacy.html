Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ahmed Kamel ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Spark at sparkcrm.cloud.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address and a hashed password when you register
  • Profile information: Company name, services, and outreach preferences from onboarding
  • Usage data: Prospect data, messages, activity logs, and pipeline information you enter
  • Session data: Basic session info to keep you logged in

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Service
  • To personalize message drafting tools to your profile
  • To authenticate your account
  • To respond to support requests and send service updates

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Google Sheets infrastructure secured by Google's enterprise-grade security measures.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sheets / Apps Script: Data storage and backend processing
  • OpenRouter / Google Gemini: Text generation for message drafting
  • Serper API: Web search for company research
  • ImgBB: Image hosting
  • GitHub Pages: Website hosting

5. Data Security

Passwords are stored as SHA-256 hashes and never in plain text. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at contact@sparkcrm.cloud to exercise these rights.

8. Cookies

Spark uses browser sessionStorage to maintain your login session. This is cleared when you log out or close your browser. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business professionals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions? Contact us at contact@sparkcrm.cloud.
